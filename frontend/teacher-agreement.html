Teacher Agreement

Draft platform policy for teacher participation

This page is a draft platform policy page, not a final legal contract. Teacher terms should be reviewed before public marketplace launch.

Teacher Responsibilities

Teachers should provide accurate teaching, respectful conduct, timely communication, and course/class support within approved platform rules.

Verification and Approval

Teachers may be required to submit qualification, experience, KYC, payout, and subject information before approval.

Platform Conduct

Teachers should not ask students for private payments, unsafe contact, or off-platform communication where platform rules require official channels.
