Student Safety

Safe communication and learning conduct

CC Academy aims to keep student learning professional, respectful, and platform-controlled.

Safe Communication

Students should communicate through official platform channels. Private contact outside the platform should be avoided for learning, payment, complaint, or teacher communication.

Verified Teachers

Future teacher marketplace features should use teacher approval, qualification checks, and KYC/document review before public teaching access.

Complaint Support

Students should report unsafe behavior, inappropriate communication, payment concerns, or class-quality issues through official support channels.
